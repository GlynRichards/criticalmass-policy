# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 25, 2026_

This privacy policy describes how the Critical Mass app (“the App”) handles your information.

## Data We Collect

**Critical Mass** does **not** collect personally identifiable information directly from users.

The game uses the following Apple services:

### 1. In-App Purchases (StoreKit)
- Purchases and unlocks are managed through Apple’s StoreKit. No payment or personal data is handled by the app.

### 2. Game Center (Online Multiplayer)
- Multiplayer features use Apple’s Game Center for authentication, matchmaking, and turn submissions.
- Game Center may collect identifiers and gameplay data in line with Apple’s privacy policies. The app does **not** store or transmit this information to any third party.

### 3. Local Storage
- Game preferences and unlock statuses are saved on your device only (using UserDefaults).
- This information is not shared externally.

### 4. Advertising
- The app may display ads. If a third-party ad network is used, some data such as device identifiers (not your name or email) may be collected by the ad provider. Refer to the ad partner's privacy policy for details.

## Children’s Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us.
