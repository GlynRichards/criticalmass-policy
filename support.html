Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Tap a cell to add mass to it. When a cell reaches its critical mass it explodes, spreading into its neighbours and capturing them – which can set off chain reactions. Eliminate all of your opponent’s cells to win. The in-game Tutorial on the main menu walks you through everything.

I bought Premium but ads are showing / my purchase is missing

Open Options → About and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

Online multiplayer isn’t working

Online games use Apple’s Game Center. Check that you are signed in to Game Center in the iOS Settings app and that you have an internet connection.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
